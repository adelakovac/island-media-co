ISLAND MEDIA CO BRAND KIT

Creative idea
The Public Frame represents the advertising space itself. Two opposing coral corners hold a simple lowercase I. The mark works on buses, screens, printed panels, web interfaces and fitter mobile screens without locking the company to one media format.

Core use
Use Carbon and Warm White for most surfaces. Use Signal Coral for primary action, brand emphasis and the frame motif. Do not use white text on Signal Coral for small copy. Carbon text on Signal Coral passes WCAG AA for normal text.

Logo files
- island-media-primary.svg: default logo for light backgrounds
- island-media-primary-reverse.svg: colour logo for Carbon or Atlantic backgrounds
- island-media-mono-carbon.svg: one-colour dark logo
- island-media-mono-white.svg: one-colour white logo
- island-media-mark.svg: compact symbol for small placements
- island-media-mark-reverse.svg: compact symbol for dark backgrounds

Minimum size
- Full logo: 160 px wide on screen, 35 mm wide in print
- Mark: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print

Clear space
Keep clear space equal to the width of the central I on every side of the mark. For the full logo, use at least the height of the MEDIA CO. line.

Do not
- Stretch or rotate the logo
- Recolour the mark outside the approved palette
- Put the logo on a busy image without a solid holding area
- Add gradients, shadows or outlines to the logo
- Replace the central I with another symbol

Implementation
The design-system folder contains CSS custom properties and JSON tokens for the management interface, client portal and fitter app.
